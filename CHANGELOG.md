# Changelog

All notable changes to NiagaCore are documented in this file. The project follows semantic versioning where practical.

## [Unreleased]

## [2.12.0] - 2026-09-03

### Added

- Admin control center for merchant lifecycle, payments, payouts, releases, incidents, support, and team access.
- Accounting-period reporting boundaries and scoped multi-branch reports.
- NIA model/dataset governance, drift monitoring, scheduled evaluation, and operational health.
- QRIS initialization, recovery, expiry, reconciliation, and structured channel errors.
- Multi-business, multi-branch, multi-device, server-side sale drafts, and operational push notifications.

### Changed

- Unified wallet account verification and manual payout evidence flow.
- Expanded role-aware operational modules, database contracts, pgTAP tests, and Maestro critical paths.
