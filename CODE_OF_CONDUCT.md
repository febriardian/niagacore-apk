# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NiagaCore welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and experience level. Contributors are expected to keep project spaces safe, professional, and focused on collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and discuss ideas, not personal traits.
- Give actionable feedback and accept technical disagreement in good faith.
- Protect private information, credentials, and customer data.
- Credit the work of others and follow repository licenses.
- Help maintain an accessible environment for new contributors.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or restrict access when needed to protect the community. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
